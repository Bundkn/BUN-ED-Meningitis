# BUN-ED-Meningitis
# Clinical and Laboratory Characteristics of Patients with Suspected Meningitis Admitted to the Emergency Department

## 1. Introduction

Meningitis is a potentially life-threatening condition that requires prompt recognition, appropriate diagnostic evaluation, and timely treatment in the Emergency Department (ED).

This study aims to describe the clinical and laboratory characteristics of patients presenting with suspected meningitis to the Emergency Department of the University Medical Center Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

## 2. Study Objectives

- To describe the epidemiological and clinical characteristics of patients with suspected meningitis admitted to the Emergency Department.
- To characterize laboratory findings, including blood tests and cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) analysis.
- To describe microbiological findings and the distribution of identified meningitis etiologies.

## 3. Study Design and Setting

| Characteristic | Description |
|---|---|
| Study site | Emergency Department, University Medical Center Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam |
| Study design | Retrospective descriptive cross-sectional study |
| Study period | January 1, 2023 – December 31, 2025 |
| Study population | Patients with suspected meningitis admitted to the Emergency Department |
| Data collection | Retrospective review of medical records |

## 4. Statistical Analysis

Data management and statistical analyses are performed using R.

The statistical analysis includes:

- Descriptive statistics for demographic and clinical characteristics.
- Categorical variables presented as frequencies and percentages.
- Continuous variables presented as mean and standard deviation or median and interquartile range, depending on data distribution.
- Description of CSF profiles, microbiological findings, and the distribution of meningitis etiologies.
